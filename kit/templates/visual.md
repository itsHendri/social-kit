# Visual

Written <date> from the website and the interview. The values live in `tokens.json`; this file says how to use them.

## Logo
Which file goes where: light and dark backgrounds, small sizes, avatars. Files are in `assets/logo/`. Never redraw or recolour it.

## Colours
Each colour, what it's for, and pairs that work (text on background). Text should keep a contrast of at least 4.5:1 against what's behind it.

## Type
Fonts for headings and for text, where the files are (`assets/font/`), and the sizes used on images and video.

## Images
What a good photo or image looks like here. What to avoid.

## Text on images and video
Font, size, position, how many words at most, and where it must stay clear of each app's buttons and captions.

## Thumbnails and covers
Face or no face, how many words, the layout, the feeling. Examples the owner likes, linked or saved in `assets/image/`.
