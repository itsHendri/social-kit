# Interview

The questions setup asks, grouped by the file they fill and in the order they're asked. Ask three or four at a time. Where the website or past posts already suggest an answer, offer it as a draft to confirm. Each round ends with its file written.

## 1. Basics → `brand.md`
- What's the brand called, and what is it, in one sentence?
- Is it you as a person, a business, or both?
- Website address.
- Timezone, and the language and spelling to write in (for example British or American English).
- A short slug for file names (lowercase, hyphens), such as `maria-lopes`.

## 2. Goals → `brand.md`
- What should social media do for you, in order? (For example: find clients, sell a product, grow a YouTube channel, build a following, stay in touch with people you know.)
- After seeing a post, what should someone do? (Follow, visit the site, watch the video, book, buy, send a message.)
- How will you know it's working? Name something you can count that matters more than views: enquiries, sales, subscribers, sign-ups.
- How much time a week can you give it, honestly?
- What have you tried that didn't work, or that you didn't enjoy?

## 3. Audience → `brand.md`
- Who are you talking to? Describe one or two real people if you can.
- What do they care about, struggle with, or want?
- Words they use. Paste comments, DMs or emails if you have them: their exact words are worth more than a description.
- Who is it not for?

## 4. Topics → `identity/voice.md`
- What are the three to five things you talk about? (Your work, how you make it, tips, behind the scenes, opinions, life around it.)
- Anything you never talk about?
- What can you show? Client work, work in progress, your face, your home, your process?

## 5. Voice → `identity/voice.md`
- Three words for how you sound, and a sentence on each.
- Anyone whose voice you'd like yours to be close to?
- First person ("I") or a team ("we")? Short sentences or long? Emoji? Hashtags?
- Words and phrases you use a lot.
- Things you'd never say, or that make you cringe in other people's posts.
- From the posts you're proud of: what makes them sound like you?
- Offer the list of common AI writing habits in `kit/lessons.md` (the "Writing that reads as AI" part). Ask which ones they want to avoid. Keep only the ones they pick.
- Hook types: show the menu in `kit/lessons.md`. Which ones feel like them, and which feel fake?

## 6. Look → `identity/visual.md`, `identity/tokens.json`
- Confirm the colours and fonts found on the website, and what each colour is for.
- Logo: which version goes where (light and dark backgrounds, small sizes)?
- Photo and image style: what does a good image of yours look like? Anything you avoid?
- Text on images and videos: font, size, where it sits, how many words at most?
- Thumbnails and covers (if they make video): face or no face, how many words, the feeling. Ask for two or three thumbnails they like, theirs or anyone's.

## 7. What you make → `brand.md`
- What do you make today? Long YouTube videos, short videos, photos, carousels, screen recordings, Remotion animations, writing?
- How often, roughly?
- Do you have older content worth reusing (videos, blog posts, a talk, a newsletter)?
- Do you edit video yourself? With what? Do you use Remotion, and where is the project?
- Do your videos have transcripts or captions files? (YouTube Studio can export them.)
- Which accounts have a history of posts? Those get audited next (`kit/audit.md`), before the channel questions.

## 8. Channels → `channels/<platform>.md`
First: which channels do you want to use? Don't suggest any until they've answered. Then, for each one:
- Why this channel: what is it for, compared with the others?
- How many posts a week, at most? (A ceiling, not a target. An empty slot is better than a filler post.)
- Which days and times?
- Which formats? (For example on Instagram: reels, single images, carousels, stories.)
- What has worked here before, and what hasn't?
- Hashtags here: yes or no, and how many?
- Does one post go to several channels, or does each channel get its own?

## 9. How you work → the `plan-posts` skill
- How far ahead do you want posts planned? A week, two weeks, a month?
- How do you want to review drafts? All at once, or as they're written?
- After you post, will you tell Claude the link so it can mark it posted?
- Anything that must happen before a post is ready (a check, someone else's OK, a tag)?
